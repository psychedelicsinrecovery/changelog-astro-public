# 🧭 Roadmap

Where PIR's GitHub home (the hub, this AstroLog and the helpdesk portals) is headed, in rough order.
What's already done is in the [changelog](https://psychedelicsinrecovery.github.io/changelog-astro-public/changelog),
and what keeps it all running is on the [sustainability](https://psychedelicsinrecovery.github.io/changelog-astro-public/sustainability)
page. This is also the repository's `ROADMAP.md`. A.L.E.X. keeps
[its own roadmap](https://psychedelicsinrecovery.github.io/alex-desk-public/roadmap.html).

## ✅ Just landed

- 🧭 **One navbar on every page**: the hub, the AstroLog, A.L.E.X. and every desk, with an icon on
  every link, folding into "More" and then a Menu on smaller screens instead of wrapping.
- 💬 **One window for every way to reach PIR®** (CONTACTS.md, both contact forms, info@), and one
  for where the fellowship gathers (Discord, committee channels, WhatsApp threads).
- 🗺️ **A [map](https://psychedelicsinrecovery.github.io/changelog-astro-public/map)** of every place
  these pages lead, every desk included, each with its own picture.
- 💜 **A [donate page](https://psychedelicsinrecovery.github.io/donate/)** that points to PIR's own
  7th Tradition page, with a thank-you list for those who'd like to be named.
- 🖼️ **A share card of its own for every desk**, so a desk's link arrives looking like that desk.

## 🔜 Next

- 📝 **The real words about giving.** PIR® is self-supporting under its traditions: it can't accept
  outside contributions, but it can accept donations, and other organizations have sponsored the
  journey. The wording is being written alongside the privacy policy, the shop, books and merch; a
  marked placeholder holds its place on `/donate/`.
- 💬 **Every committee's Discord channel** in the community window, as each committee names its
  channel publicly (LitCom's `#🖨lit-com` is there now).
- 👛 **Give from a crypto wallet** on `/donate/`: one button for browser wallets, phone wallets and
  email sign-in. The slot is there now, empty on purpose.
- 🔒 **Live private rows on the ledger**, once a read-only `LEDGER_TOKEN` is added.

## 🌌 Later, and still being explored

- 🙏 **Thanks you can claim yourself:** a contributor could add their name to the contribution ledger
  by signing with a wallet, with no personal details involved.
- 👋 **A welcome from PIR's founder.** The welcome guide keeps a space for a few words from Kevin F,
  empty on purpose until he'd like to write them.
- 🕸️ **A network of our own.** A small social space owned by the fellowship, on open protocols like
  Lens and Farcaster, paired with a DAO and run by group conscience, anonymity first. A direction,
  not a promise.
