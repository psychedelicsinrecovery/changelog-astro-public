PIR's logos now have an official home on GitHub

Medium 2026-10-04GitHub home & brand 1 min readBy Christopher W
TL;DR: PIR's logos and brand guidelines now live in one public place — psychedelicsinrecovery/brand-assets — with published literature to follow, so nobody has to dig through website media or personal drives.
Read the full story →

Official files used to be scattered across the WordPress media library and individual volunteers’ drives. GitHub is becoming PIR’s single source of truth for media:

  • brand-assets — the PIR® logos (legacy versions for now), plain-language brand guidelines for the PIR® and Psychedelics In Recovery™ marks, and a roadmap for clean vector versions.
  • Literature (coming next) — the 2026 editions of PIR’s published pieces (Steps, Traditions, Preamble, Guiding Principles, FAQ, Safety Guidelines, meeting script) with stable links the website and A.L.E.X. can point to.

Shared in Discord, WhatsApp or iMessage, it arrives with its own preview card and its own star.
