Online Meeting Guide: closed meeting removed and links refreshed

Medium 2026-11-09main site 2 min readBy Christopher W + Littlebird
TL;DR: Four approved fixes are live on the Online Meetings page: the closed Tuesday UK Step Study is removed, Family of Origin and Friday Topic/Discussion now use calendar-confirmed direct Zoom links, and Salt Lake City's displayed time matches the calendar at 4:30 pm PT / 7:30 pm ET.
Read the request, changes and verification

The request

Douglas Nelson asked in the Tech Committee WhatsApp thread for the Tuesday UK Step Study to be removed because it had shut down. Christopher reported that the Zoom calendar was already fixed and asked Littlebird to clean up stale links below the embedded calendar. This starts the dedicated Online Meeting Updates thread, following the in-person maintenance workflow. Christopher approved all four verified fixes on November 9.

The four edits

  • Removed the Tuesday UK Step Study listing, previously 1 pm PT / 4 pm ET, Zoom meeting 835 3934 1211. The November 9-15 calendar instances did not include it. Wednesday’s separate Step Study remains unchanged.
  • Replaced both Family of Origin links to the old tuesday-family-of-origin registration page with the direct Zoom URL supplied by the November 10 calendar instance, meeting 818 0602 6923. Its listed time remains 6 pm PT / 9 pm ET.
  • Replaced Friday Topic/Discussion’s old friday-night-topic registration-page link with the direct Zoom URL supplied by the November 13 calendar instance, meeting 865 1902 6921. Its listed time remains 5 pm PT / 8 pm ET.
  • Corrected Salt Lake City hybrid from 5:30 pm PT / 8:30 pm ET to 4:30 pm PT / 7:30 pm ET, matching the November 13 calendar instance. Its Zoom link is unchanged.

Verification and scope

  • Calendar source: the page’s embedded zoom.main.pir@gmail.com calendar. No calendar events were edited in this session.
  • Target: WordPress page 31, Elementor text-editor widget 5a3e269. Took a server-side export snapshot before editing: emcp-content-mirror/page-31-online-meetings.json.
  • Re-read the widget immediately after saving: its stored editor HTML exactly matched the prepared surgical patch, with unrelated content unchanged.
  • Fetched the public page with a cache-busting query and verified the removed UK listing, both new direct Zoom destinations, and the corrected Salt Lake City time. Published and verified November 9 at approximately 1:58 pm Eastern.
  • Glasgow and San Francisco had no matching calendar instances through December 31 in the name searches. Absence is not proof of closure; both listings remain untouched pending confirmation.
