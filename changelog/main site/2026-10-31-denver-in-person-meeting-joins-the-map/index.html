How it landed →

📮 The request

  • The meeting’s contact, writing as ~DΔV, asked Rick to get the new Denver meeting on the website (Kevin M., who handled such things before, is winding down his web duties — so it came to TechCom). Rick passed it to Christopher with the location, time, format and length.
  • Two details took a quick back-and-forth to pin down: the meeting is on Wednesdays, and it runs 75 minutes. The public contact line uses ~DΔV’s handle rather than a guessed name.

🔧 The edit

  • The In-Person page is a classic-HTML post (post 11150) — one unparsed content blob, not Elementor blocks — so this was a surgical insert, not a rebuild. The new entry copies the house style (centered heading, the muted slate-blue address block, the long divider line) and lands in alphabetical order: Santa Cruz, CA → Denver, CO → Chicago, IL.
  • Before touching anything, a server-side export snapshot was taken (emcp-content-mirror/page-11150-in-person-meetings.json) and the original 22,770-character content was pulled from the database for a local diff.
  • The pushed content was verified byte-for-byte: identical to the original everywhere except the three added Denver lines. (Two invisible &nbsp; spacer characters in pre-existing empty paragraphs came back as plain spaces — a rendering-identical quirk of the round-trip, left as is.)
  • The live page was re-fetched after the update and the Denver entry renders correctly in place.

🫙 A note on the pipes

  • The site’s own MCP bridge (emcp) was briefly down for this session — the WordPress.com connector was tried as a detour, but it only reaches personal WordPress.com blogs, not the org’s site. The bridge woke up on its own a few minutes later and the whole edit went through it: read, snapshot, update, verify.
