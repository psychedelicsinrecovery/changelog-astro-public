PR, October 11, 2025: the podcast gets its runway

Medium 2025-10-11PR Committee 2 min readBy PR Committee (Anne R chair)
TL;DR: The PR Committee of Psychedelics In Recovery™ – PIR® puts the podcast on its runway: the pilot is recorded by Karin and Topher, edited by Ian Yeh, production moves to Riverside, and launch is targeted for November 1. The [De Vine News](/changelog-astro-public/changelog/newspaper/) newsletter takes its first real shape - a Step of the Month, an announcement square for every committee - with editorial standards written down: anonymity, no dosing, right to edit.

In the room

Anne R, Topher (Bliss, SoCal) and Dave present; Karyn S joined during the call. Apologies from Missy (unwell) and Kevin (who sent written comments on the press release). David L uncertain and likely absent.

The podcast

  • The pilot was recorded by Karin and Topher and edited by Ian Yeh; production moves from Zoom to Riverside for quality.
