Episode 3 launch

The poster circulated; the episode went live on Spotify, Apple Podcasts and YouTube the same day. Direct links preferred over “link in bio.” Future launches follow one rhythm: WhatsApp poster, Instagram static, a short caption, an email insert, and a website page - all the same day.

De Vine cross-referencing

Each newsletter edition gets a podcast highlight box with a direct listening link and a short quote.

Social media strategy

A content calendar; a recovery-forward, not psychedelic-forward, tone; quote cards; audiograms later; and a monthly content tracker spanning podcast, meetings, convention, newsletter and announcements.

Convention promotion (Denver 2026)

Messaging aims at ten years of PIR® - integration, community depth, safety and education. Potential speakers tracked in the speaker spreadsheet.

Governance

A standardized workflow: clear asset ownership, documented release procedures.
