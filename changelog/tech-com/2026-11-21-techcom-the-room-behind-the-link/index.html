The room behind the link

Medium 2026-11-21Tech Com 3 min readBy Tech Committee
TL;DR: The Tech Committee mostly works where people cannot see it: a Zoom link for Ann Arbor's hybrid meeting, a new Zoom link for Fellowship of the Spirit, a corrected Salt Lake City listing, and three WhatsApp community groups - Zoom Concierge for questions, Meetings Happening Now for postings, and Fellowship of the Spirit for its thread. The theme underneath: TechCom exists to facilitate people already doing good work and make their jobs easier.
Unfold the story of the room

Filed for Psychedelics In Recovery™ – PIR® from the November 21, 2026 GSR Subcommittee meeting, the chair’s final minutes, and the TechCom thread. Some TechCom detail stays at the tech desk by design - this entry is the room’s story, not its whole contents.

The concierge bell rings both ways

Karyn’s minutes record her asking that Jess serve as admin of the meetings-now group, recognizing the posting she was already doing. For the record: Jess was already made admin of Meetings Happening Now when the group was created. She has done exactly this kind of stewardship in other fellowship chats for years, so the minutes’ phrasing reads as recognition, not a to-do. In the room, Karyn S also thanked members who post meetings there so nobody walks a lonely room alone.

Karyn S flagged Zoom Concierge for GSRs to share with their groups: a WhatsApp line for anyone whose question is technical - how to share a screen, how to mute, how to chair without the tech going sideways. She asked GSRs to carry it home to the technically challenged zoomers among their members, noting that the concierge cheat sheet already prepared for the chat is there for anyone who wants it.

In the supplied TechCom conversation, Rick B credited David K with starting Fellowship of the Spirit, and David welcomed a coordination space that could help if a host cannot attend. It is a simple example of continuity becoming a shared task.

From answering questions to back-end support

Christopher and Kevin F plan to connect soon, and among the first orders of business is empowering Christopher to provide the back-end support that concierge conversations sometimes escalate into - helping hosts with matters beyond questions, on call. That is coordination underway, not a staffed desk yet; it is exactly the facilitation TechCom exists for, making the work of people already serving easier.

Christopher also comes to this from live audio and video production - an audio engineer and technician by trade - and is eager to help hybrid meetings find their footing as time unfolds. PIR’s convention conference gave live-streaming its first real test. The craft exists in the fellowship already; hybrid could yet be the start of something, the way the Traveling GSR role was.

One more invitation from Christopher: the fellowship’s Discord continues to grow, including on-demand parking-lot rooms. Around the holidays, PIR has liked to run day-long parking lots - a room open for anyone who needs a meeting during a season once spent using with friends or drinking with family, whether for connection or quiet, at any point of the day. Details for this year are being clarified with Erin B; check current announcements before planning around it.

The quiet list

The TechCom thread itself stays a working room - fix logs, link checks, host coordination, the unglamorous inventory that keeps Sunday morning working. That is deliberate: the fellowship trusts the desk to make sure everything works and to be there when it does not, which is its own kind of service readiness.
