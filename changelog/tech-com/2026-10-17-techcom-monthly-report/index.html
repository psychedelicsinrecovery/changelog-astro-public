Submitted for the October 17, 2026 GSR meeting; also filed here so the record and the report are the same document.

What we worked on

  • The PIR Helpdesks are live. Type /techcom, /litcom, /github, /pr or /mod anywhere in Discord to open a private thread with the right team; the Helpdesk Portal serves non-Discord members; A.L.E.X. answers questions from PIR’s own pages, with sources.
  • The AstroLog Changelog is live as the fellowship’s service record, with a desk for every committee, including a new GSR desk gathering minutes and reports. Minutes on Google Drive are migrating in, retroactive entries included, with original sources kept linked.
  • We repaired a 24-hour publishing outage in the changelog pipeline and hardened it with a leak check so private service notes never reach the public site.
  • The Convention Hub and Galleries reflect the concluded gathering, and recorded sessions are on the PIR YouTube channel.
  • A lingering CDN issue on psychedelicsinrecovery.org was traced to its root cause with our host and resolved (WPX ticket #517528).
  • A new walkthrough teaches any fellowship member how to contribute images to the record, no coding needed.

How to reach us

TechCom is on-call via WhatsApp rather than hosting regular meetings: come find us in the WhatsApp threads, or open a /techcom ticket. If you want to learn GitHub service, we teach; if you already know it, we would love your hands.
