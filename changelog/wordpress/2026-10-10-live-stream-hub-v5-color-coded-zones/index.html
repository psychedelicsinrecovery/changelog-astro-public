Live Stream Hub v5: color-coded time zones, one-click day cards, and a new schedule poster

Medium 2026-10-10WordPress sites 2 min readBy Christopher W
TL;DR: The Convention live-stream page got its biggest polish yet: every session now lists color-coded PDT / EDT / BST / NZDT times, each day image + title + button is one big clickable card, the "To watch" instructions are a clear 3-step list, and a new sacred-medicine schedule poster (San Pedro, ayahuasca vine, wachuma blooms) went live — including the social preview image.
Read the full story →

Time zones, color-coded

  • Every session in the day modals now carries a sub-line with PDT (cyan), EDT (orange), BST (violet), NZDT (green) under the gold Denver chip — the same code the printed poster uses.
  • Modal headers now say “other zones listed per session.”

One container per day

  • The three separate click targets (image, title, button) are merged into a single large card per day: image, title, and a day-colored button (Friday flame red-orange, Saturday violet, Sunday sunset pink — matching the day artwork). One click anywhere on the card opens the schedule & Zoom rooms.

Clearer “To watch” instructions

  • The one-paragraph instruction became a 3-step list: tap a day card → find your session and “Join on Zoom” (with registration email handy — waiting room + email sign-in protect live sessions) → recordings will be hosted on the PIR YouTube channel after the convention.
  • Modal footers updated to match: recordings language replaces the old “select sessions also stream live” line.

New schedule poster (v3)

  • Sacred-medicine art nouveau frame: San Pedro left, ayahuasca vine right, wachuma blossoms, calm navy center — schedule stays the star.
  • Day headers colored to match the web day cards; trademark standardized to Psychedelics In Recovery™ – PIR®.
  • Featured image + Yoast og:image now serve the new poster (verified live in WhatsApp previews with v2; v3 inherits the same path).

Under the hood

  • Yoast indexable cache had pinned the old featured image (14702); fixed via re-save, og:image now attachment 14718.
  • Mobile keeps the full-viewport (100vw/100vh) modal treatment so schedules stay readable on phones.

Shared in Discord, WhatsApp or iMessage, it arrives with its own preview card and its own star.
