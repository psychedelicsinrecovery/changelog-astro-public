2026 meeting scripts: the stale twelve-steps link gets fresh hyperlinks

Low 2026-11-14Lit Com 1 min readBy Christopher W + Littlebird
TL;DR: David Karabelnikoff spotted a stale hyperlink to the Twelve Steps inside the 2026 suggested meeting script PDFs on the Member Materials page. The hyperlinks on pages 2 and 4 dated from the original publish. Christopher rebuilt the documents in Apple Keynote and replaced the media on WordPress the same evening - the Main Meeting Script and the combined suite PDF are both live with fresh links.
Read the full story →

The report

On the evening of November 14, David Karabelnikoff messaged Christopher in WhatsApp: while helping with a meeting, he had noticed the meeting guide on the website carried an old, outdated link to the Twelve Steps of Psychedelics In Recovery™ - PIR®. He shared the document he was reading - PIR_Suggested-Meeting-Script-2026.pdf, four pages - and asked whether the links could even be updated. “I don’t know if the links can be updated, but wanted to let you know.”

They could. Christopher recognized it immediately: the 2026 LitCom materials, published to the Member Materials page as one of his first acts as TechCom chair, had been rendered from Apple Keynote - and the hyperlinks on pages 2 and 4 of the source documents were never refreshed when the files were published.

The fix, same night

  • Christopher updated the hyperlinks in the Apple Keynote source documents and used upload-and-replace to swap the media on WordPress - no filename churn, same URLs.
  • Verified fixed on both the Main Meeting Script PDF and the whole-suite-in-one-file combined document: PIR-Scripts-2026_combined.pdf.
  • Confirmed live at approximately 8:12 pm Eastern, November 14.

Why it matters

The Twelve Steps are what these scripts point people to, meeting after meeting. A link that goes somewhere stale is a small thing that every reader hits alone. A fellowship member paying close enough attention to flag it - and a chair who fixed it within the hour - is the service structure working exactly as drawn. Thank you, David.

Shared in Discord, WhatsApp or iMessage, it arrives with its own preview card and its own star.
