Member materials, same words, new clothes: reformat prototypes

Medium 2026-11-22Lit Com 1 min readBy Christopher with Littlebird
TL;DR: Visual reformat prototypes for Netiquette Statements, Cares & Considerations, and Guidance on Sponsorship are built. Every word of the authoritative source PDFs is preserved, verified by an ordered word-by-word comparison. A few source quirks were found and kept; one item is flagged for LitCom.
What was built and what was checked

The prototypes

Three PDFs, one per document, in the 2026 polished house style: EB Garamond serif, PIR navy headings, the official diamond-and-wordmark logo centered at the top of the first page, and a faint PIR diamond watermark behind the text. The sponsorship document carries its source attribution on every page: PIR Sponsor Info 11-2021, Howard W., website advisor, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.

Wording verification

The text of each prototype was extracted and compared, word by word and in order, against the authoritative PDF currently published on psychedelicsinrecovery.org. Result: all three documents match. The only tokens that do not appear are extraction artifacts in the sources themselves (a word split as “se ek” in the original).

Source quirks, kept on purpose

  • The Table of Contents says “Structure and Support” and “Spirituality and Sustainability”, but the body headings read “Structure & Support” and “Spirituality & Sustainability”. The original files differ this way; the prototypes preserve the difference.
  • The body heading “Reminder of Risks and Research” is shortened to “Risks and Research” only in the Table of Contents. Preserved as-is.
  • Two resource URLs keep their original tracking fragments (a Facebook click ID on the Healing Maps link and a Verywell Mind highlight fragment). They are preserved verbatim for fidelity, though LitCom may prefer to trim them to clean canonical links in any adopted version.

Status

These are prototypes for the Literature Committee to review. Nothing has been adopted, and nothing has replaced the current files on the website. The designs are a proposal of what Git-backed, polished, downloadable member materials could look like.

Shared in Discord, WhatsApp or iMessage, it arrives with its own preview card and its own star.
