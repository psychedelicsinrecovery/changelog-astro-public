LitCom October report: the book launch, old minutes on the record, and a fellowshipping month

Submitted for the October 17, 2026 GSR meeting; also filed here so the record and the report are the same document. Attendance at the October 13 meeting: Megan P, Titus and Christopher W.

What we worked on

  • The book launch is on the site. “Living Psychedelic Sober” is now woven through PIR’s web properties: the Twelve Step pages carry the new artwork and links to the book.
  • Old minutes are coming onto the record. Historical Literature Committee minutes have been populated to the AstroLog, with the 2025-2026 gaps to be filled in soon. Minutes on Google Drive are migrating to the changelog, retroactive entries included, with original sources kept linked.
  • The second FAQ batch is in the works. Building on the first batch from the 2025 member-materials work, LitCom will develop a second batch of FAQ questions for the main site as part of the member materials. (See the member-materials teaser for the story so far, including the second FAQ lineage found already live on the site.)
  • A fellowshipping month. Committee work resumes its regular rhythm; most of October went to supporting the convention and each other.

Next meeting

The Literature Committee meets the second Tuesday of the month: the next meeting is Tuesday, November 10, 5:00 PM Pacific / 8:00 PM Eastern. All are welcome.
