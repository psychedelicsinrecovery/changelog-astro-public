Filed for Psychedelics In Recovery™ – PIR® from the November 21, 2026 GSR Subcommittee meeting, the chair’s final minutes, and Christopher’s updated agenda.

A committee that wants company

Christopher did not sugarcoat it: the Literature Committee meets on the second Tuesday, and lately almost nobody shows up. The time has been used as a meditation-and-connection meeting instead. “I didn’t want to get too far into it without more heads looking at it, so that it doesn’t get one or two voices.” That is the whole reason this entry exists - the work is real, the queue is long, and more hands would mean more of it can move without resting on the same few shoulders.

The November agenda, explicitly

So that people can prepare ahead, the working table as reported at GSR and in Christopher’s updated agenda:

  1. Second FAQ set. Reconcile the newer set of questions already on the website with the earlier member-materials FAQ document, question by question.
  2. Timeline and history. Update the timeline link at the foot of the Board of Directors page and connect it with the history page.
  3. Nonprofit best practices. Draft a reader-friendly digest or white paper covering nonprofit best practices, board structure and bylaws education points, with FAQs on bylaws and board structure.
  4. Bylaws links. Refresh the bylaws links in the footers of both the main site and the service subdomain.
  5. Privacy policy. Review the privacy policy pages on the main site and the service subdomain; the privacy-policy group now sits as a LitCom subcommittee.
  6. Convention disclaimers. Add the disclaimers needed now that the convention integrated WooCommerce and related tools.
  7. Document care. Reformat Netiquette Statements, Cares & Considerations and Guidance on Sponsorship for consistency - wording untouched; a visual refresh is never permission to change substance.
  8. Readiness. If the groups adopt it, help review and align the proposed Recovery Readiness for Service document as the group-conscience process develops. No adoption or publication approval was given at GSR.

And this queue is the beginning, not the end: there is more work behind it once these items move. A first plain-language parking-lot explainer is being drafted for committee review - a draft only, pointing to the existing Safety Guidelines rather than creating policy.

Christopher’s post-meeting notes also describe this work as preparation for what comes after WordPress - living, Git-backed sources for member materials that a future infrastructure can render - and note that the privacy policy work, which the fellowship received so warmly, came out of exactly this kind of small, quiet committee. The queue above is where a new member could find a first small commitment.
