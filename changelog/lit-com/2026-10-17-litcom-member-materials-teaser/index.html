2025: building the member materials

The Literature Committee spent 2025 working through PIR’s member material resources - the first batch of FAQ questions, printed materials, and the Twelve Steps poster that carried the fellowship’s words into meeting rooms.

2026: the trademark era

2026 became the year of implementing the Psychedelics In Recovery™ - PIR® trademark into these documents, aligning the fellowship’s written voice with its registered identity.

The FAQ twist

When TechCom keys arrived to upload the committee’s work, two discoveries: the first batch of FAQ questions we had worked on needed updating on the front end - and the front end was already carrying a whole other set of FAQs, found buried in the LitCom Google Drive, that had been sitting on the site for us to sit with. Two FAQ lineages, one website.

What’s next

The second batch of FAQ questions is now in the works for the main site, destined for the member materials. This entry will grow into the full 2025-2026 story as the source documents are mirrored in.

Shared in Discord, WhatsApp or iMessage, it arrives with its own preview card and its own star.
