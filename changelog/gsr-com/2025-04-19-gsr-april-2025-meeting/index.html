Source: GSR Agendas 2025, April 19, 2025 section (mirrored into GitHub; original stays linked).

The meeting

Tradition of the month: Tradition 4 - each group autonomous except in matters affecting other groups or PIR as a whole. A summary of the Intergroup meeting preceding this was given.

Old and new business

  • GSR reports: is your meeting open or closed? Answers still outstanding from some meetings.
  • Prism’s proposal: a mediation committee for the fellowship.
  • Cheryl’s proposal: a name change for the Book Committee.
  • Podcasts: the PR committee asked fellows to recommend recovery and/or psychedelics podcasts, and to flag conferences, seminars and festivals in their neighbourhoods.
  • MAPS Denver, June 16-20, 2025: who is going? A WhatsApp group formed for coordination.
  • Rotation: Anne’s commitment as IG and GSR committees chair reached 14 months and came due for rotation.

Reports and action items

  • A new in-person PIR meeting in Berlin needed to be added to the calendar.
  • Time-zone fixes for physical meetings, discussed in March, still open - so events appear at the correct local time.

Next GSR meeting: Saturday, May 17, 2025.
