Sixteen groups report in: attendance, motions and open service roles

Medium 2026-09-19GSR Committee 3 min readBy Karyn S
TL;DR: The September GSR written reports, recorded as submitted: sixteen groups checked in. Attendance ranged from 2 in Ann Arbor to about 40 at Sunday Integration. Sunday Integration passed three motions at its 8/30 business meeting, and the open service roles worth filling this quarter are chair, greeter, facilitator, time keeper and GSR positions across eight groups.
Read the full story →

Source: minutes filed by the chair (Karyn S), shared through the GSR WhatsApp thread. Written reports are recorded as submitted; last initials only.

Written reports

  1. San Diego In Person (Evan K, GSR) - Attendance: 18. Open positions: speaker seeker. Status: recent format change to improve the flow of the meeting, mostly rearranging blocks rather than significant text changes. Note: members routinely say how important and lovely attending this meeting is for them.
  2. Ann Arbor In Person (Jodi H, GSR) - Attendance: 2. Open positions: none. Status: struggling with attendance over the summer, with minimal attendance for the last 6 to 7 weeks. Needs: support with attendance and getting the word out for new members. Note: a few new people joined the meeting chat group; she hopes they will come to the meeting.
  3. Thursday OG (Sue W, GSR) - Attendance: 10 to 15. Open positions: chair soon. Status: doing fine.
  4. Salt Lake City (Shaynna, GSR) - Attendance: 5. Open positions: none; positions are on rotation. Status: excited for the convention; the whole group of regulars will be attending. Announcement: the group still needs its meeting time changed to Fridays at 7pm MST.
  5. Wednesday UK/Europe (Sam K, GSR) - Attendance: 20. Open positions: time keeper, male greeter. Status: doing fine, regular attendance, most service positions filled.
  6. Monday Entheon (Rick B, GSR) - Attendance: entered as “Regularly” (no number). Open positions: none. Status: doing fine.
  7. Glasgow Journey Through Self (Ami L, GSR) - Attendance: 5. Open positions: none. Status: struggling with attendance at the meeting and wants to attract new members.
  8. London (Anne R, reporting; the group does not have a GSR) - Attendance: 6. Open positions: GSR. Status: went back to monthly meetings in August due to poor attendance on a weekly basis.
  9. Sunday Integration (Paula G, GSR) - Attendance: 40. Open positions: greeter. Status: three motions passed at the 8/30 business meeting:
    • Motion 1 (format change): adds a pause at 8:55 PM to share the 7th Tradition invitation to make a contribution via the link pasted in the chat. When a QR code becomes available, it will also be shared on the screen at that time.
    • Motion 2 (format change): removes wording around discouraging shares when in an altered state.
    • Motion 3 (procedural): business meeting changed to the third Sunday of the month, following the Saturday GSR Subcommittee meeting.
  10. Friday 11th Step Mysticism (Andria, GSR) - Attendance: 22. Open positions: facilitator, chair. Status: just needs to get a new chair; otherwise a great meeting.
  11. Thursday Liminal Lounge (Andria, GSR) - Attendance: 30. Status: “an ongoing moving meeting.” Announcement: “This is a PIR Group..” (as submitted).
  12. Friday Night Topic (Scott E, GSR) - Attendance: 20+ ish. Open positions: none. Status: doing fine.
  13. Monday Womxn’s Meeting (Ari H, GSR) - Attendance: 10 to 20. Status: doing fine.
  14. Saturday Morning Live (Ben, GSR) - Attendance: 30. Open positions: chair. Status: making a format change (no details given).
  15. Wednesday Queer in Recovery (Ben, GSR) - Attendance: 5. Open positions: none. Status: doing fine.
  16. Thursday Love, Sex and Relationships (Jaime I, GSR) - Attendance: not reported; the GSR has not been able to attend due to life circumstances. Open positions: okay for now, will need a host soon. Status: thinks it is going okay but has not had a pulse on it. Announcement: please attend the business meeting. The group has been trying literature again and this month read from four books - Is it Love or Is it Addiction; How to Be an Adult in Relationships; The SLAA workbook; How to Get the Love You Want - and will decide this week which to read.

Open service positions (from written reports)

  • Thursday OG: chair soon
  • Wednesday UK/Europe: time keeper, male greeter
  • London: GSR
  • Sunday Integration: greeter
  • Friday 11th Step Mysticism: facilitator, chair
  • Saturday Morning Live: chair
  • Thursday Love, Sex and Relationships: host soon
