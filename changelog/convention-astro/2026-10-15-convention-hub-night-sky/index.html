Convention Hub night-sky restyle; home CTA repointed; MCP is back

High 2026-10-15convention-astro 1 min readBy Christopher W
TL;DR: The WordPress MCP came back and the queued styling work landed: the Convention Hub page now wears the Night Sky layout (hero, pills, six-card grid, closing section), the home page's "View the Announcement" button points to /convention-hub/, and a galleries-propagating teaser sits under it.
What changed →

🖥️ The Convention Hub page (ID 14756)

The plain bullets became a night sky:

  • Container carries the deep-space gradient (#0B0918 → #06050E), centered column, 1180px wide.
  • Hero: gold kicker pill (“The gatherings of Psychedelics In Recovery™ – PIR®”), the Convention Hub heading, lede, and three info pills (dates, place, in-person & streamed).
  • Two pill buttons: a violet→gold gradient “See the 2026 Schedule & Program”, and an outlined “Browse the Photo Gallery” — now pointing at the fellowship’s own gallery (psychedelicsinrecovery.github.io/gallery).
  • A six-card grid: Schedule & Program · Live Stream · Photo Gallery · The Story (GitHub site) · Events Calendar · Get Involved (Convention Committee + 7th Tradition). Dark panels, 18px radius, gold links.
  • Closing section: past & upcoming gatherings plus the photo-consent note.

🏠 The home page (ID 9572)

  • “View the Announcement” now opens /convention-hub/.
  • Beneath it, a quiet italic teaser: the photo galleries are propagating to their new fellowship-owned home at gallery.psychedelicsinrecovery.org.

🧩 Notes for the next pair of hands

  • The emcp MCP was down all morning; a re-ping restored it. When it’s down, queue the exact element IDs and settings — the reconnection is quick.
  • Parallel writes to one WordPress document race and clobber each other (three of six card containers were lost and had to be re-added). Write sequentially to a single page; batch across pages only.
  • Headings are lowercased by the Astra theme’s global CSS — inline text-transform:none keeps Night Sky casing.
  • WPX/CDN served the fresh page immediately; no cache purge needed after these edits.

Shared in Discord, WhatsApp or iMessage, it arrives with its own preview card and its own star.
