The gallery wall, the album switcher, and the lightbox that hid in plain sight

High 2026-10-16convention-astro 3 min readBy Christopher W
TL;DR: Round three, same day. The stray "download button" at the page bottom turned out to be the lightbox itself: its CSS was scoped to .pir-conv but the overlay lives outside it, so it rendered as an unstyled stub while silently locking the scroll. Unscoped, it works. The Convention gallery hero became a gallery wall - framed, tilted tiles with a warm spotlight and a live "photos gathered" chip - and clicking a tile now hands you to its album: Choose-an-album is a real switcher (a tab per album, ready for the Convention's many albums; a stage that previews the selected album as thumbnails; thumbnails open the lightbox). About fits one row; anchor jumps stop short of the navbar; the walkthrough's consent note moved beside the reach-out; the main gallery kept "click me 1st" a little longer.

🕯️ The lightbox was there all along

Christopher reported the lightbox “not rendering, but something is running because page scroll stops” - and a download button sitting weirdly at the bottom of the page. Both were the same bug: the overlay’s CSS said .pir-conv .conv-lb, but the overlay’s div lives outside .pir-conv. The selector never matched, so the overlay rendered as a plain block at the page bottom (its top bar: count, Download, Close - the mystery button), while the JavaScript happily added the .open class and locked body scroll. One unscope fixed both.

🖼️ The gallery wall

The Convention gallery hero now hangs its marquee tiles like framed prints - white mats, alternating tilt, a warm spotlight - and carries a wall-chip that reads the manifest: “1 album gathering - the first photo could be yours”. Clicking a tile hands you to its album below.

🗂️ The album switcher

Choose-an-album is no longer a static grid. It is a tab per album (the Inaugural Convention will have more than one - the design waits for them), a stage that previews the selected album as a thumbnail grid, and thumbnails that open the lightbox. Albums still gathering show their Pixieset and walkthrough paths. A frontmatter-const gotcha (is:inline scripts cannot see MANIFEST) briefly broke the whole thing; the URL is inline in the script now.

🧹 The small repairs

  • Anchor jumps get scroll-padding-top: titles no longer hide under the fixed navbar.
  • The kit footer’s four groups (Explore, Helpers, Get involved, About) fit one row: the 11.5rem grid minimum was wrapping About at wide views.
  • The scroll cue lifts above the bottom dock, and on the gallery hero sits inline above the marquee.
  • Marquee pagination math is honest: pages = floor((track width - viewport)/viewport) + 1, not round(width/viewport).
  • Walkthrough: “See the albums” and “The repository” buttons centered; the photo-consent note now sits directly under “Not sure which path fits?”; the walkthrough button inherits the Convention CTA’s glow, and the Convention button adds a bounce.
  • Main gallery: pills centered, manifest fetch retries once, and the empty state explains itself when viewed locally (file:// cannot fetch the manifest - open the live gallery instead).
  • “Click me 1st” stays on the main gallery pages a little longer while visitors learn the two sites apart; the Convention site keeps “Neighborhood Nav”.

The record of the git dance: Christopher’s gallery commit landed clean but was rejected for push (the remote had moved); the rescue is reset-hard to origin, re-curl the corrected files, commit, push. The convention-astro social-card commit just needs pull –rebase first.

Shared in Discord, WhatsApp or iMessage, it arrives with its own preview card and its own star.
